# Item spot art

Item spot art: nine official Wizards of the Coast illustrations used under the Fan Content Policy, plus one original using the requested filename stems. Original download dimensions are preserved. The three transparent AI-assisted extractions are 1254px square. No WebP conversion was applied.

Blackrazor and the Vecna relics have AI-assisted cutouts; the two downloaded source images are in `references/`. Other images are unchanged downloads. Some official item designs differ from the descriptive brief: Vorpal Sword has a red blade, Holy Avenger is silver/blue, Staff of the Magi is a simple forked staff, and Orb of Dragonkind is green and ornamented.

Source artwork retains its existing copyright; no new reuse license is granted by this download.
