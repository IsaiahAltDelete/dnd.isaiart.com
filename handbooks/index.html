Codices & Forbidden Tomes

The Library of IsaiartThe Volumes

Longest volumes first. Several of these run to some two hundred pages apiece.

Supplemental codices and forbidden tomes: the volumes I keep scribing when by rights I ought to be running the session. Every one of them may be read within your browser, page by page, as a proper handbook should be. Some are rules and some are lore, and one is very nearly a jest; all are set in the manner of the old hardbound books, with tables, sidebars and stat blocks where they belong.

Leave at the gate a book the keep has never held, and you may read whatever you can carry in your head.

Of the Wizard’s Handbook

The Forgotten Realms Wizard Handbook, the 2025 wizard set down in full with its class core and its practice of the Art across the Realms, no longer stands upon the shelf alone. It is now bound into The Weave & Beyond, where it keeps company with the warlock, the sorcerer and the artificer.

The old quick reference for the table has been retired, for the handbook holds all that it held and more besides.
