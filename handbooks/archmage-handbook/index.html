Introduction

A vintage 5e-style handbook of the great wizard names that still shake worlds: Elminster, Mordenkainen, Khelben, Szass Tam, Alustriel, Laeral, Karsus, Vecna, Iggwilv, Bigby, and Gale of Waterdeep. Each profile is presented in 5e format, with official 5e material used where it exists and lore-faithful 5e conversion work used where the game never printed a full modern sheet.

In This Codex

  • 5e format throughout
  • Profile art for every wizard
  • Official spellbooks plus legacy signatures
  • Attire, relics, towers, sanctums, and lairs

Reading a Codex Entry

Each archmage fills a chapter of two pages. The first page tells the wizard's story: portrait, summary, vital particulars, the long journey of deeper lore, and the threads that define it. The second shows the wizard at home and at war: attire, quarters, a personal read, and a full statistics block with the spellbook beside it. Legacy signatures are separated from current 5e spellbook entries so the profiles stay rules-clean without losing history. Spell names appear in italics; tap a spell level in any stat block to fold it away.

A Note on Sources

This codex uses official 5e sources where available and clearly marked 5e conversions where modern published sheets do not exist. The goal is table usefulness without flattening the long history of these archmages into generic spellcaster blocks.

A wizard's power is measured in spells. A wizard's legend is measured in what the world still remembers after the spells are spent.
