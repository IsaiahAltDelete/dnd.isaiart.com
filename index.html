Well met, traveller. Herein is gathered all that I have forged for the gaming table: instruments for the making of heroes, engines of chance that conjure monsters and cities at the turn of a die, the lore of the planes, and a shelf of hand-bound tomes. No coin is asked and no ledger kept. Every page may be read freely in your browser, while the world beyond the window keeps your own hours, from dawn until the witching hour.

Every map has an edge, and every edge a door. The trick of it is knowing which book to open first.
