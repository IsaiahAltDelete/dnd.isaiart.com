# SWORD COAST CHRONICLES — Setting Bible (Forgotten Realms)

**Read this before writing any name.** Everything in the game is drawn from published
Forgotten Realms canon. Do not coin generic fantasy names. When you need a name that
isn't in this list, follow the naming conventions below.

Year: **1496 DR**, the Year of the Purple Dragons. Region: the **Sword Coast North**,
between Waterdeep and Neverwinter. The campaign starts in **Phandalin**.

---

## 1. THE HUB: PHANDALIN

A frontier town in the foothills below the Sword Mountains, rebuilt on the ruins of
an old Netherese-era settlement. Muddy streets, a well, timber-and-fieldstone houses,
Tresendar Manor's burnt shell on the eastern rise.

| Building | Run by | Purpose in game |
|---|---|---|
| **Stonehill Inn** | Toblen Stonehill (Trilena, wife; Pip, son) | Rest / long rest / rumours / recruit board |
| **Barthen's Provisions** | Elmar Barthen (clerks Ander & Thistle) | General goods, adventuring gear |
| **Lionshield Coster** | Linene Graywind (guard Harbin's men) | Weapons & armour |
| **Shrine of Luck** (Tymora) | Sister Garaele — Harper | Healing, remove curse, revivify, holy items |
| **Phandalin Miner's Exchange** | Halia Thornton — Zhentarim | Ore/gem sales, Zhent contracts |

| **Alderleaf Farm** | Qelline Alderleaf, Carp Alderleaf | Halfling farmstead, druid contact |

## 2. THE REGION (overworld map)

Running roughly west→east and north→south:

- **Triboar Trail** — the main east–west road. Phandalin sits south of it.
- **High Road** — north–south coastal road: Waterdeep → Leilon → Neverwinter.
- **Neverwinter Wood** — vast pine forest, Thundertree ruins on its edge, ash from Mount Hotenow.
- **Sword Mountains** — Cragmaw Castle, Icespire Peak, Wyvern Tor, Old Owl Well (Netherese ruin).
- **Kryptgarden Forest** — green dragon Claugiyliamatar's wood, south of the Trail.
- **Mere of Dead Men** — drowned salt marsh on the High Road, lizardfolk and will-o'-wisps.
- **Conyberry** — ruined village, Agatha's grove nearby.
- **Thundertree** — ash-choked ruin, Venomfang's tower, twig blights, Cult of the Dragon.
- **Leilon** — small rebuilt town on the High Road.
- **Neverwinter** — the City of Skilled Hands, Protector's Enclave, Blacklake District.
- **Waterdeep** — City of Splendors; the **Yawning Portal** tavern (Durnan) drops into…
- **Undermountain** — Halaster Blackcloak's endless dungeon. The never-ending content.
- **Wave Echo Cave** — the Lost Mine of Phandelver, the Forge of Spells.
- **Cragmaw Hideout / Cragmaw Castle** — goblinoid strongholds, King Grol.
- **Anchorite hills, Starmetal Hills, Ardeep Forest, Dessarin Valley, Goldenfields** — outskirts.
- Underdark reaches below: **Sorcere/Menzoberranzan** drow raiding parties (high tier).

Biome ids to use in code: `road`, `plains`, `forest`, `pine-forest`, `hills`,
`mountain`, `marsh`, `coast`, `ruins`, `cave`, `dungeon`, `crypt`, `mine`,
`ash-waste`, `tundra`, `underdark`, `city`.
