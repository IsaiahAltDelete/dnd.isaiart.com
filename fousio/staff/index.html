On the staff

Grows With You

+1 staff, and Shield once per Long Rest. Level 5: +1 Spell Save DC; Counterspell (1 charge) refunds the countered spell's level. Level 9: +2 staff, +2 Spell Attack, Retributive Strike.

Resonant Ward

A 2nd-level+ spell or any Abjuration spell adds 1 charge to your Arcane Ward (the spell's level from level 9).

Soul Charges

10 Soul Charges (20 at level 5, 30 at level 9), partly regained each Long Rest. On the last charge: 5% surge, 5% grief.
