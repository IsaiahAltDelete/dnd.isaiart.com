# Fousio models

The GLBs live in `fousio/assets/models/`; this folder holds the script that makes them.

Web copies of two items from *Fousio's Tome of Items*, Isaiah's Baldur's Gate 3 mod.

Every `.glb` here is glTF 2.0 binary, Y up, metres, with its bbox centred on the origin
and its front facing +Z. Node transforms are baked (nodes carry none). Attributes are
float32 POSITION + NORMAL with uint16 indices. There are no UVs, textures, skins,
animations or compression.

| file | item | tris | size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fixed-star.glb` | Staff of the Fixed Star (star at +Y) | 6,684 | 175 KB |
| `circlet-a.glb` | Circlet of Nous A (wide), polished | 21,009 | 440 KB |
| `circlet-b.glb` | Circlet of Nous B (peak), polished | 26,766 | 505 KB |
| `circlet-c.glb` | Circlet of Nous C (lens), matte | 20,425 | 386 KB |
| `circlet-e.glb` | Circlet of Nous E (open), polished; D is the same mesh, matte | 39,437 | 812 KB |
| `circlet-f.glb` | Circlet of Nous F (straight), polished | 31,518 | 583 KB |
| `circlet-g.glb` | Circlet of Nous G (straight, open), polished | 26,024 | 481 KB |
| `circlet-i.glb` | Circlet of Nous I (loop), polished; H is the same mesh, matte | 16,860 | 313 KB |

`circlets.json` maps all nine looks (stats entry, in-game name, mesh) to their file and
finish, and gives the factors of both finishes.

The circlets sit as worn: each ring rises 12–22° toward the brow, like vanilla circlets.
`wornTiltDeg` in `circlets.json` is the exact angle per file. In three.js,
`rotation.x = wornTiltDeg * Math.PI / 180` levels the ring.

## Where they come from

**fixed-star.glb** comes from the BG3 Forge model `bg3forge/models/lib/staff-gold-star.js`,
exported with the forge's own `export_cli.js --glb` in modelling space (not
`--bg3-weapon`). After export it is:

- centred, since it stood on its spike at y 0;
- given readable material names from the forge palette, which the in-game textures also
  use;
- given `KHR_materials_transmission` 0.70 and `KHR_materials_ior` 1.55 on the amber gem,
  taken from `js/materials.js` (the forge's own writer can only express this as alpha
  blending).

In the source model the gem is completely enclosed by the star boss, so it never shows.
Skipping node `star-gem` spares three.js a transmission pass.

**circlet-\*.glb** are the meshes the mod actually ships:
`modworkshop/isaiahsartifacts/src/Generated/Public/IsaiahsArtifacts/Assets/Dizzy/Braille_Crown_*_HUM_M.GR2`,
generated by `modworkshop/braillecrown`. Each one was traced from its stats entry through
the RootTemplate and the human-male visual to its GR2 and material, then processed:

- **Conversion:** converted to DAE with LSLib Divine and taken in bind pose, with the
  skin dropped (the crowns are rigid on Head_M).
- **Parts:** each file has two parts, `crown-body` and `braille-dots`.
- **Orientation:** BG3's game space is left-handed, so the conversion applies z → −z,
  which removes the mirror and turns the face to +Z, and reverses the winding to match.
- **Normals:** normals that the GR2 split by less than 1° are welded back together.
  Under 1 % of triangle corners had a normal 60–150° off their own face; these showed as
  dark bars across the ribbon tops, in game too, and now take the normal of their smooth
  group.
- **Material:** from the mod's `Crown_Gold` / `Crown_Gold_Matte`. The basecolor DDS
  (240 grey) × the `Metal_Primary` dye (1, 0.612, 0.311) gives baseColorFactor
  (0.860, 0.527, 0.267). Metallic is 1. Roughness from the PM DDS is 0.254 (polished) or
  0.651 (matte).

## Regenerate

```
python make_models.py --out ../../assets/models              # everything, then verify
python make_models.py staff --out ../../assets/models        # or: circlets / verify
```

Needs `node`, LSLib's `Divine.exe` (the Granny-enabled copy in
`~/Documents/BG3Modding/Packed/Tools`) and the ClaudeStuff workspace
(`~/Documents/ClaudeStuff`). Override those paths with `FOUSIO_DIVINE` /
`FOUSIO_CLAUDESTUFF`. The script uses standard-library Python and only reads its sources.
Output is byte-identical from run to run.
