A thin band that keeps the mind in one piece. Braille is cut into the gold, so a blind wearer can read the ward with a fingertip.

Grows with its bearer

Legendary

On the circlet

Measured Focus

+1 to Spell Attack Rolls while you are concentrating; from level 5 the bonus is permanent; from level 9 also +1 Spell Save DC.

Sharp Mind

From level 5: +1 to Constitution Saving Throws.

Unshakable Mind

From level 9: Advantage on Concentration Saving Throws.

Nine looks

AFour ribbons that merge and part, opening into lens shapes at the temples. Polished gold.
BThree ribbons that loop the head together, the top one parting into a low lens over the brow. Polished.
CThree ribbons that loop the head together, parting widest over the brow and merging behind the ears. Matte gold.
DMatte gold that leaves the forehead bare: the ribbons sweep round the sides and back and end at the temples in two soft wings. The matte twin of E.
EPolished gold that leaves the forehead bare: the ribbons sweep round the sides and back and end at the temples in two soft wings. The polished twin of D.
FThree straight ribbons round the head, the middle one broad and lettered in braille, polished gold.
GThe straight triple band with the forehead left bare: three ribbons round the sides and back, ending at the temples. Polished gold.
HOne ribbon, no end: it runs round the back as the upper band, turns at each temple and comes back as the lower one, the forehead left bare. Matte gold, braille on the upper run.
IOne ribbon, no end: it runs round the back as the upper band, turns at each temple and comes back as the lower one, the forehead left bare. Polished gold, braille on the upper run.

Earlier forms

Circlet of Nous (Restored) Rare

The fractured band has been made whole, and every repaired line points thought toward purpose.

Circlet of Nous (Legendary) Very Rare

The crown of a finished theorem: light, severe, and almost impossible to distract.

Circlet of Nous (Fragmented) Uncommon

A thin study circlet set with a small brass flower to draw wandering thoughts into line.

In the final save he still carried the Fragmented circlet; the evolving circlet came later, in the run these photos are from.
