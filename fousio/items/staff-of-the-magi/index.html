Staff of the Magi

Quarterstaff · 1d6 Bludgeoning
Damage1d6 Bludgeoning
Versatile1d8
PropertiesMelee, Versatile
SlotCarried
RarityLegendary
Weight1.8 kg
Value6,400 gp
SourceFousio's Tome of Items (Isaiah's own mod)

An elder staff of perilous repute, bright with warded fire and hungry for hostile magic. Wakes at levels 5 and 9.

Properties

  • +1 weapon enchantment
  • +1 weapon enchantment from level 9
  • +2 weapon enchantment

Effects

Dormant

+1 staff, +1 Spell Attack, Magelight, Spell Absorption, 20 charges.

Awakened (level 5)

Level 5: +2 Spell Attack, Spellguard, 35 charges, more spells.

Legendary (level 9)

Level 9: +2 staff, 50 charges, the full spell list and Retributive Strike.

Arcane Might

The staff sharpens your casting, granting +1 to Spell Attack Rolls (+2 once Awakened).

Spellguard

Once Awakened (level 5), its wards stand between you and hostile sorcery, granting Advantage on Saving Throws against spells.

Spell Absorption

When an enemy casts a spell that targets only you, you can use your Reaction to make the staff absorb it: the spell fails and the staff gains a number of charges equal to the spell's level, up to its maximum of 50.

Magelight

The staff sheds a steady magical light while equipped.

Grants

LightAction

Infuse an object with an aura of light.

Mage HandAction

Create a spectral hand that can manipulate and interact with objects.

Arcane LockAction · 2nd level

Close a door or container with a magical lock. It can no longer be lockpicked or opened with Knock.

Detect Magic (Arcane Sight)Action · 2nd level

The deeper sight once named Detect Magic is rendered here as Arcane Sight.

Protection from Evil and GoodAction · 1st level

Protect an ally against the attacks and powers of aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, fiends, and undead.

Enlarge/ReduceAction · 2nd level

Make a creature larger or smaller to affect their weapon damage and Strength Checks and Saving Throws.

Flaming SphereAction, 2 charges · 2nd level

Summon a flaming sphere that damages nearby enemies and objects. You can move the sphere. It emits a bright light.

InvisibilityAction, 2 charges · 2nd level

Touch a creature to turn it Invisible. Attacks against it have Disadvantage. It attacks with Advantage.

KnockAction, 2 charges · 2nd level

Unlock an object that is held shut by a mundane lock.

WebAction, 2 charges · 2nd level

Cover an area in thick, flammable webbing that slows creatures within and possibly Enwebs them.

Dispel Magic (Remove Curse)Action, 3 charges · 3rd level

Lift a curse from a creature or object. Stands in for Dispel Magic, which has no equivalent in Baldur's Gate 3.

Fireball (Level 7)Action, 7 charges · 6th level

Shoot a bright flame from your fingers that explodes upon contact, torching everything in the vicinity.

Lightning Bolt (Level 7)Action, 7 charges · 6th level

Call forth a blast of lightning that hits all creatures in the line of the eruption.

Ice StormAction, 4 charges · 4th level

Impel a storm of hail and ice to crash from the sky, covering the ground and striking all objects and creatures within range.

Wall of FireAction, 4 charges · 4th level

Create a blazing wall of fire, burning anyone who dares stand too close.

TelekinesisAction, 5 charges · 5th level

Throw a creature or object up to 18m with a thought. Once per turn, you can use Telekinesis again without expending a spell slot.

Conjure ElementalAction, 7 charges · 5th level

Bend the barrier between the Planes until they disgorge an elemental ally to follow and fight for you.

Passwall (Arcane Gate)Action, 5 charges · 6th level

Open two linked portals that let you and your allies pass where walls and chasms would stop you. Stands in for Passwall, which has no equivalent in Baldur's Gate 3.

Plane Shift (Banishment)Action, 7 charges · 4th level

Shunt a creature to another plane of existence for 10 turns unless it succeeds on a Charisma Saving Throw. No Concentration is needed. Stands in for Plane Shift, which has no equivalent in Baldur's Gate 3.

Retributive StrikeAction, 10 charges

Break the staff over your knee. It is destroyed, and its remaining magic detonates in a 9 m blast. Choose how many charges to unleash: every other creature in range must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity Saving Throw or take Force damage equal to 6 times the charges (half on a success). You have a 50% chance to slip to another plane and escape the blast; otherwise you take Force damage equal to 16 times the charges.
