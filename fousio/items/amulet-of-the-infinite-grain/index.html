Amulet of the Infinite Grain

Amulet
SlotAmulet
RarityVery Rare
Weight0.05 kg
SourceFade's Assorted Treasure Expansion

Sand steadily slips down the glass tube, each grain marking the passage of time as if it were nothing.

Effects

Temporal Defiance

The wearer exists slightly outside the normal flow of time, granting them immunity to becoming Slowed.

Temporal Acceleration

Time bends to hasten your steps, granting a +3m bonus to movement speed and a +2 bonus to Initiative rolls.

Grants

SlowAction · 3rd level

Target up to 6 creatures and Slow them. Movement and actions are affected, and their AC and Dexterity saves are reduced by 2.
