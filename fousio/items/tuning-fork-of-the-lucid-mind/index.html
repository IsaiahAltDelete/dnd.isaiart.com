Tuning Fork of the Lucid Mind

Instrument
SlotInstrument
RarityUncommon
Weight0.02 kg
SourceFade's Assorted Treasure Expansion

This steel fork is perfectly balanced. When struck against a hard surface, it emits a low, unwavering tone that vibrates through the skull, sharpening the user's thoughts into a singular point of focus.

Effects

Lucid Frequency

The hum of the tuning fork lingers in your mind, drowning out distractions. You gain a +1 bonus to Spell Attack Rolls and Spell Save DC.
