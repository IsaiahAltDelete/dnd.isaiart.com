Robe of the Archmagi

Armour
SlotArmour
RarityLegendary
Weight1.8 kg
Value6,000 gp
SourceFousio's Tome of Items (Isaiah's own mod)

Worn by the great wizards of the high schools — those who lived long enough to claim the title and short enough to still have something to prove. The silk is stiff with old ink, the seams reinforced with a thread no mundane needle could carry. Stitched into the hem in faded gold are the names of every spell its previous owners ever bothered to write down.

Properties

  • Armour Class becomes 15 + Dexterity modifier

Effects

Archmagi's Grace

The robe braces every weave with a higher one. Your Armour Class is set to 15 + your Dexterity modifier. Gain +2 to Spell Attack Rolls, +2 to Spell Save DC, and Advantage on Saving Throws against spells.
